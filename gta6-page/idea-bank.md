# Idea bank

Ready-to-go drafts by pillar. Each draft has a status:

- **READY**: no facts to check. Can go out once approved.
- **NEEDS FACT**: uses a claim that must be in the *Verified* table of `facts.md` first (the fact ID is noted).
- **NEEDS MEDIA**: waiting on an image or clip in `media/`.

Every good question from comments goes here too (under Questions or HOW TO).
Don't repeat a caption or one-liner within a week (playbook section 7).
