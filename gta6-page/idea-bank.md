# Idea bank

Ready-to-go drafts by pillar. Each draft has a status:

- **READY**: facts are verified (or there are none). Goes out once the owner approves.
- **NEEDS FACT**: uses a claim that must be in the *Verified* table of `facts.md` first (the fact ID is noted).
- **NEEDS MEDIA**: waiting on an image or clip in `media/`.

Every post goes to **TikTok + Instagram** (same video, captions adjusted). Every good question from comments
goes here too. Don't repeat a caption or one-liner within a week (playbook section 7).

**Video format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 7–30s. Hook text on screen in the first second. Burned-in captions.
AI voice (text-to-speech in CapCut, TikTok or Instagram) reads the voice script. Lower the original audio
under the voice; don't erase the creator's audio entirely.

After launch, source tips from: repeated questions in our comments, r/GTA6, official Rockstar Support
articles. Only post steps we've seen work in footage or an official source. No exploits or glitches that
break the game's rules.

